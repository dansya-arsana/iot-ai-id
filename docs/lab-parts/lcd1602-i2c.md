# LCD1602-I2C

Protocol: `i2c` slave at 0x27 (PCF8574 backpack) in front of an HD44780 16x2 controller in 4-bit mode.

- Device (simulation): `apps/web/src/lab/sim/devices/lcd1602-i2c.ts`
- Part (3D): `apps/web/src/lab/scene/parts/lcd1602-i2c.ts`
- Test: `tests/lab-part-lcd1602-i2c.test.ts`
- Pins (backpack order): GND, VCC, SDA, SCL

PCF8574 byte: P0=RS, P1=RW, P2=E, P3=backlight, P4..P7=D4..D7. The controller latches on the falling edge of E. Models: 3x 0x3 then 0x2 init, function set, display/cursor/blink, entry mode, clear, home, DDRAM addressing (line 2 at 0x40), display and cursor shift, 8 CGRAM glyphs, read cycles (address counter, data). Busy flag is always ready. Inputs: backlight colour, contrast.

Provenance: own work from the public HD44780 and PCF8574 datasheet behaviour; the 5x7 dot font is the public-domain classic table; dimensions are datasheet mm; no vendor art or logos.
