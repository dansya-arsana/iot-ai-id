# WATER-LEVEL

Protocol: `adc` (an analog sensor: the device puts a voltage on an ADC pin).

- Device (simulation): `apps/web/src/lab/sim/devices/water-level.ts`
- Part (3D): `apps/web/src/lab/scene/parts/water-level.ts`
- Test: `tests/lab-part-water-level.test.ts`
- Pins: VCC, SIG, GND

Output rises 0 to 2.4 V with a slightly concave curve as the traces are covered, our simplification, not measured data. Provenance: own work, no vendor art, photos or logos.
