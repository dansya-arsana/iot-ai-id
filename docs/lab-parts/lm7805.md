# LM7805

Protocol: `gpio` (a power part: no signal pins, it only takes part in the net solver, see docs/lab-extending.md "Power").

- Device (simulation): `apps/web/src/lab/sim/devices/lm7805.ts`
- Part (3D): `apps/web/src/lab/scene/parts/lm7805.ts`
- Test: `tests/lab-part-lm7805.test.ts`
- Pins: IN, GND, OUT (breadboard columns 0, 1, 2)

Provenance: own model and behaviour from the public LM78xx datasheet facts (TO-220 outline 10.2 x 4.5 mm, 5 V +-4 %, dropout about 2 V, 35 V max input). No vendor model, logo or art.
