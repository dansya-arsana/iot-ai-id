# BH1750 light sensor (GY-302 module)

Protocol: `i2c`. Emulated.

- Device: `apps/web/src/lab/sim/devices/bh1750.ts`
- Part: `apps/web/src/lab/scene/parts/bh1750.ts`
- Test: `tests/lab-part-bh1750.test.ts`
- Catalog record: `packages/catalog/data/bh1750.json`
- Pins (module order): VCC, GND, SCL, SDA, ADDR (holes 0-4)

Model: address 0x23 with ADDR low or open, 0x5C with ADDR at or above 0.7 x VCC (read from the ADDR net). Opcodes 0x00 down, 0x01 on, 0x07 reset (data only, powered only), 0x10/0x11/0x13 continuous H/H2/L, 0x20/0x21/0x23 one-time (then power down), MTreg 0x40-0x47 and 0x60-0x7F (31..254, default 69). Conversion time 120 ms (H, H2) or 16 ms (L), scaled by MTreg/69 (typical figures, the maximums are 180 and 24 ms). Two bytes, MSB first, count = lux x 1.2 x MTreg/69, doubled for H2; clipped at 65535. Before the first conversion ends a read returns the previous value (0 after power-on or reset). State: lux, mode, mtreg, address, ready, count, clipped, powered, reads, writes. L mode does not quantise to 4 lx steps.

## Field notes

- Outdoors it needs a diffuser dome and a rain shade. Full sun is about 100 klx: in H mode at MTreg 69 the count clips at 54612 lx, so use MTreg 31 or L mode.
- The module has a 3.3 V LDO and I2C pull-ups, so 3-5 V works. The bare chip is 2.4-3.6 V.

- Board size: 18.5 x 13.9 mm (https://probots.co.in/light-intensity-bh1750-sensor-for-arduino-raspberry-pi-gy-302.html) or 19.25 x 14.4 mm (https://github.com/Erriez/ErriezBH1750) [U]. We use 18.5 x 13.9.
