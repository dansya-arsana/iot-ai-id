# MG996R metal-gear servo

Protocol: `pwm (desk prop)`.

- Device (simulation): `apps/web/src/lab/sim/devices/mg996r.ts`
- Part (3D): `apps/web/src/lab/scene/parts/mg996r.ts`
- Test: `tests/lab-motion-drivers.test.ts` (group test for stepper, ULN2003, MG996R, IRF520 and pump)
- Pins: SIG, V, GND (cable and plug, no breadboard holes)

Model: the same pulse decoding as the SG90 (544 us = 0 degrees, 2400 us = 180 degrees, valid 400-2600 us) with the datasheet 5 us dead band. Speed 0.17 s per 60 degrees at 4.8 V and 0.14 s at 6 V, stall torque 9.4 kg.cm at 4.8 V and 11 kg.cm at 6 V, both interpolated from the real supply on V (5 V when unwired). The popover has a load on the horn; above the torque the horn stays put and the state shows the 2.5 A stall current. Rated 4.8-7.2 V and flagged as needing an external supply.

Provenance: own work from public datasheet behaviour and typical module dimensions (stated in the file header of the part); no vendor models, photos or logos, nothing downloaded into the repo.
