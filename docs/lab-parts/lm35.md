# LM35

Protocol: `adc`. 10 mV per degree Celsius, basic range 0 to 150 degC on a single supply.

- Device: `apps/web/src/lab/sim/devices/lm35.ts`; part: `apps/web/src/lab/scene/parts/lm35.ts` (TO-92 body from `ds18b20.ts`); test: `tests/lab-part-lm35.test.ts` (real firmware via `tests/fixtures/sketches/thermo-analog.ino`)
- Pins: VS, OUT, GND (TO-92 from the flat face). Rating 4 V to 30 V.

The output is the ideal 10 mV/degC value, limited to supply minus 1.5 V of headroom and never below 0 V; the ADC then quantises it (4.88 mV step on a 5 V Uno, about 0.49 degC). The +-0.5 degC accuracy is not modelled. Works on A6/A7 of a Nano (`ctx.analogPin`).

Provenance: own work from the public datasheet behaviour; TO-92 geometry is our own.
