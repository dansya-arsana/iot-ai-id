# toggle-switch

SPST miniature toggle (MTS-101-style, 6 mm bushing).

## Behaviour

Mechanical contact. `switches()` joins A and B when ON. B on a board pin reads the A-side level while closed; open reads LOW (pull-down style).

## Notes

Popover toggle `on`. Tests: tests/lab-part-toggle-switch.test.ts.

## Provenance

Model and device written by us from public datasheet behaviour (generic part class). No vendor models, photos, logos or libraries.
