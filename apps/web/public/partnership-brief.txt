iot.ai.id — Indonesia hardware partnership brief

Purpose
Connect useful IoT and robotics hardware with builders, schools, makers, and engineers in Indonesia through documented experiments and practical learning.

Partnership topics
- Evaluation samples and exact board/module variants.
- Pin maps, electrical ratings, datasheets, SDKs, and example firmware.
- Pilot testing with explicit acceptance criteria and recorded failures.
- Repair documentation and reproducible retesting.
- Distribution discussions after commercial and authorization terms are agreed.

Current product
An AI-assisted hardware workspace with a hardware catalog, visual wiring canvas, structured hardware contracts, deterministic validation, and a local desktop bridge for supported ESP32 experiments.

Current limits
The public website is an introduction and documentation surface. Hardware availability, official distribution rights, active tester numbers, and verified robotics results are not asserted. The humanoid visual is a concept illustration. Raspberry Pi and robotics catalog entries do not imply full runtime support. Physical verification requires connected hardware and captured evidence.
