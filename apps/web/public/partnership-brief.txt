iot.ai.id — Real-world hardware verification partnership brief

Purpose
Test robotics, sensor and IoT hardware in real Indonesian field conditions and return machine-readable, verifiable evidence for engineering and AI training.

Why Indonesia
- Tropical field environments: heat, high humidity, brackish water, unstable power and intermittent networks.
- Diverse deployment sites: aquaculture ponds, greenhouses and plantations, cold-chain and warehouses, factory floors, smart buildings and remote telemetry.
- Local vocational electronics and mechatronics talent to operate lab benches and field sites.
- Close access to the low-cost component supply chain for comparative and clone testing.

What we offer
- Environmental stress testing: run your hand, sensor or node under defined field conditions.
- Field pilot deployments: long-running installations with telemetry, observation and failure reporting.
- Failure and recovery datasets: identity-bound records linking configuration, conditions, measurements and outcomes.

How verification works
1. Specify: hardware, target behaviour and environment are written as a machine-checkable contract.
2. Deploy: operators install the setup at a lab bench or field site following generated instructions.
3. Execute: the agent compiles, flashes, boots and measures, capturing serial output and readings.
4. Record: results, camera evidence and failures are stored as a verifiable record.

What we need from partners
- Evaluation units and exact variant identifiers.
- Electrical ratings, interfaces, SDKs and reference firmware.
- Acceptance criteria and the conditions you want tested.

Current limits
Images on the website are concept renders, not available products. Site counts, partner relationships and verified robotics results are not asserted on the public website. Evidence formats shown are examples of the record structure.
